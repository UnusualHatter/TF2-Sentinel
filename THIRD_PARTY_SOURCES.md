## Import rules

- Preserve the original source slug and raw evidence relation.
- Count mirrors and aggregators as provenance, not independent corroboration.
- SourceBans records contribute only when the reason is explicitly cheat/anti-cheat related and the ban has not been reversed as a false positive.
- MvM community reputation comments are weak signals; unrelated accusations or insults are not imported.
- VAC/game-ban signals stay separate from TF2-specific cheating convictions.
- SteamHistory is identity-history enrichment only and contributes zero confidence.
- Tom exports are treated as one reviewer-confirmed source family even when the same SteamID appears in multiple export formats.
- Compiled LMAOBox priority sections retain their named upstream category and independence group rather than being counted as one extra independent source.
